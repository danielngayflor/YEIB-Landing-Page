About YEIB

What is YEIB?

The Youth Entrepreneurship Investment Bank (YEIB) Technical Assistance Program is a Government of Liberia initiative, led by the Ministry of Commerce & Industry through its Small Business Administration, and financed by the African Development Bank. YEIB exists to help small and growing Liberian businesses become stronger, more organized, and ready for the next stage of growth.

This is the first cohort of the YEIB program. We're selecting 50 MSMEs from across Liberia to take part in a structured program of training, mentorship, and market connections. If you apply and aren't selected this time, more cohorts will follow — this is the beginning of an ongoing program, not a one-time opportunity.

YEIB is not a grant program. It doesn't hand out cash. What it offers instead is structured, hands-on support to help your business become genuinely investment-ready — the kind of foundation that makes it easier to grow, to borrow, and to attract serious partners down the line.

Female entrepreneurs and entrepreneurs with disabilities are highly encouraged to apply.

Program Support

What Will I Get?

Selected MSMEs receive structured support built around two pillars — at no cost.

Capacity Building

Hands-on training in financial management, investment readiness, business planning, and digital skills — built to help you run a stronger, more organized business.

Access to Market

Direct connections to buyers, financiers, and investors — opening doors that are hard to open alone.

This is a capacity-building and readiness program, not a cash grant. The value is in the training, mentorship, and connections — not a direct payout.

National Reach

Open to All 15 Counties

YEIB is a national program — not a Monrovia-only initiative. Entrepreneurs in Margibi, Bong, Nimba, Lofa, and the southeastern counties are just as encouraged to apply as those in Montserrado.

    At least 50% of the MSMEs selected in this cohort will come from outside Montserrado County.
